\newcommand{\ArrowLabel}[3]{%
  \begingroup\setlength{\fboxsep}{0.5pt}%
  \colorbox{#1}{\textcolor{#2}{{\arrowlabelfont\fontsize{6}{6}\selectfont #3}}}%
  \endgroup}
% All board arrows share one path; styles below only choose color, offset,
% line weight, dashes, and (where needed) a move label.
\newcommand{\BoardArrow}[5]{%
  \pgfsetstrokecolor{#2}%
  \pgfsetlinewidth{#3}%
  \pgfsetarrowsend{stealth}%
  \pgfpathmoveto{\pgfpoint{#4}{#5}}%
  \pgfpathlineto{\pgfpointadd{\pgfpointanchor{#1}{center}}{\pgfpoint{#4}{#5}}}%
  \pgfusepath{stroke}}
\newcommand{\BoardDashedArrow}[3]{%
  \pgfsetdash{{2pt}{1.5pt}}{0pt}%
  \BoardArrow{#1}{attackRed}{1pt}{#2}{#3}}
% Black moves: yellow arrow and label; White moves: blue arrow and label.
\newcommand{\BoardMoveArrow}[5]{%
  \BoardArrow{#1}{#2}{1.1pt}{0pt}{0pt}%
  \pgftext[at=\pgfpointscale{0.5}{\pgfpointanchor{#1}{center}}]{%
    \ArrowLabel{#3}{#4}{#5}}}

% Damiano's Mate
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\section{\texttwemoji{alembic} Damiano's Mate}

% 这个模式很简单，关键在于g6兵对h7后的支持，以及f8挡住王的去路（一般是易位后的车）。
% 为了达成这个局面，我们往往需要创造h开放线，并常伴随着h车弃子来给后开路。
This pattern is quite straightforward; the key lies in the support provided by the \underline{g6 pawn} to the \underline{h7 queen}, combined with the f8 square being blocked (typically by a rook from castling).

To reach this position, one usually needs to open h-file, which frequently involves sacrificing h-file rook, to clear a path for the queen.
